# Draw & Dash persona

**Status** — Approved by Group 22 on November 24, 2026, under the [story generation plan](../plans/story-generation-plan.md).

## P-01

**Sam, the casual player.** Sam wants to find out what a pet they invented will do when it meets a distraction on the track. Winning is fun, but watching their dog give up first place for a snack can make a better round. “Sam” is an illustrative name. The goals, situations, and constraints below are drawn from the team’s [vision](../../../vision.md) and [clarification record](../requirements/requirement-verification-questions.md).

**What Sam brings to a round.** Sometimes the pet starts with a detail Sam can sketch, such as one drooping wing. Other times the clearest part of the idea is a habit Sam can describe in a sentence. Sam plays on the local demo laptop. Their first chance to judge the AI’s interpretation comes when they review the pet card. The test that matters to them happens on the track: can they recognize the trait they confirmed?

| Creation situation | What Sam wants to keep or control | Relevant stories |
|---|---|---|
| Draw | Erase one mistaken stroke while preserving the rest. See the recognizable original artwork race. | US-01, US-04 |
| Type | Describe a peculiar creature without sketching. Confirm its appearance before it becomes a sprite. | US-01, US-03, US-05 |
| Speak | Say the idea aloud and correct a misheard name or habit in the transcript. | US-02, US-03, US-05 |
| Combine inputs | Draw wings and explain that they are decorative. Settle what the wings mean while keeping them visible. | US-01, US-02, US-03, US-04 |
| Try a prepared example | Reach the same pet review and race flow quickly enough to see what a habit looks like in action. | US-01, US-03, US-06 |

**During the race.** Sam wants enough time to spot the dog stopping and the rival passing, with one choice about when to cheer. Hearing a line about the pass should make it easier to follow. If the narrator is still describing the previous event, Sam needs current captions and a mute control that works mid-sentence. See US-06–US-10 in [Racing and commentary](stories.md#racing-and-commentary).

**At the finish.** Sam wants to know where the dog placed and what actually happened to it. They may replay with the same pet to try a different cheer. If a required AI operation fails, knowing which step failed and how to restart is more useful than an unexplained loading screen. See US-11 in [Results](stories.md#results), US-12 in [Recap](stories.md#recap), and US-13–US-14 in [Replay](stories.md#replay).

**Success from Sam’s perspective.** During a round with sound on, Sam hears the snack call, points out the dog dipping toward the treat, and mutes the line while the racers keep moving. Afterward, they can match the recap to the overtake they saw. This is a proposed demo observation.
